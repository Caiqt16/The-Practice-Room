The when / then cycle

Finish this sentence in your head: "When ______, then I'll rest."

Maybe you said one of these:

When the kids are older.When work settles.When life calms down.When I have more time.

Notice that one of them came to mind before you finished reading.

Here is what those sentences leave out: life rarely calms down on its own, and your body has been waiting patiently the whole time.

So try a different sentence. Say it quietly: "I'll start with one breath, right now." Then take that breath. In for 4. Out for 6.
